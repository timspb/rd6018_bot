## 1. Сначала разделить сущности
NAM (negative active mass) — отрицательная активная масса Pb/PbSO4, где при HRPSoC накопление PbSO4 на отрицательной пластине и ухудшение DCA особенно значимы; PAM (positive active mass) — PbO2/PbSO4, для ресурса существенны коррозия/рост решетки, размягчение и осыпание, а также транспорт кислоты/воды.
Традиционный expander NAM — *комплекс* лигносульфонатов, BaSO4 и небольшого количества углеродной фазы; индивидуальные роли различны: органические расширители/адсорбция меняют структуру Pb и рост кристаллов, BaSO4 способствует nucleation PbSO4, carbon влияет на электронный контакт, морфологию и double-layer capacitance. Отсюда нельзя объяснить весь эффект одним «сажа проводит ток».
Не смешивать NAM-additive, PAM-additive, углеродный токосъёмник и lead-carbon гибрид с конденсатором — это разные архитектуры.
DCA (dynamic charge acceptance) — ток, реально принимаемый в кратком динамическом режиме при данном SOC,T, предыстории; НЕ C20, CCA, charge efficiency, lifetime или отсутствие газовыделения. HRPSoC microcycle — короткий микроцикл в частичном SOC; он НЕ равен полному заряд-разрядному циклу и не равен сроку службы в годах.
Известные побочные реакции: повышение скорости HER при неподходящем carbon увеличивает расход воды и ухудшает coulombic efficiency. В VRLA кислородный цикл и доступная вода осложняют компромисс. PAM углерод может окисляться и терять свойства — нельзя считать его общим средством «ремонта» старой батареи.

## 3. Lessons from three independent experiment styles
- Bozkaya S01 varied **external carbon surface area at fixed 2wt% NAM** and found short-pulse DCA/CA rises then plateaus. More BET/porosity cannot be linearly extrapolated. Long run-in and standstill changed rankings.
- Xie S03 varied **dose of one carbon-foam preparation at 2C/50% SOC**; nonmonotonic optimum 1% with large between-dose differences. Does not prove 1% best for another carbon/formulation or aging duty; result is microcycles count, not C20.
- NCC S06 varied **nitrogen functionalization**; authors reported reduced HER (interpretation) and improved cycle microcycles but conditions not matched to S03; neither 4324 vs 3642 nor 5.3x vs 2.9x permits rank across laboratories.
- S08 shows in some HRPSoC matrices lignin changes outperform carbon fibers; a negative plate 'expander cocktail' is multi-component and a source of confounding.

## 5. Safety / engineering boundaries
Carbon is introduced into NAM/PAM during industrial paste preparation and cell formation. Cannot retrofit it into a sealed or flooded used battery by adding powders to electrolyte or forcing high-voltage charging. None of these publications establishes a user charging-current setting to reproduce carbon benefits. In VRLA additional hydrogen/oxygen effects and water inventory demand more stringent safety and validation. Lab HRPSoC high-rate pulses are NOT transferable to Leoch 72Ah or RD6018 as a test program.
Differentiate cell construction (NAM-limited 2V, AGM 2V, EFB 12V, flooded), SOC/T/SG, negative additives/barium sulfate/lignin, manufacturer and batch, geometry/acid reserve/operating duty. This is a science input about *battery manufacturing* as well as charge dynamics, not charger retrofit.

## 6. Experiments for future controlled scientific validation (no hardware action)
P17-A: paired lab cells with the same grids/paste-process/forming, 0-carbon vs several characterized carbons at controlled **per-cell factory additivation**, ideally with batches. Measure BET external vs total area, PSD particle size/porosity, electronic conductivity, PbSO4 phase SEM/XRD, K–K checked EIS, independent DCA standard + run-in, C20 at common T/I/end-U, H2 evolution and water loss, thermal gradients. Before/after rest/age and independent long-run acceptance.
P17-B: factorial NAM expander design carbon-type×dose×lignosulfonate×BaSO4 under fixed electrolyte reserve/SOC; otherwise false attribution to carbon. Quantify covariation and interactions, not single optimal concentration per all battery categories.
P17-C: PAM carbon materials separately under 2V AGM reference; monitor oxidative stability, porosity, adhesive/shedding, capacity & water loss, oxygen recombination, positive grid corrosion. Not possible to claim PAM improvement from a NAM DCA test.
P17-D: 2V→12V scale verification; manufacturer/material holdout; charge histories and cycle-to-cycle drift, strong comparison to simple baseline. Wh/Ah cycles and true equivalent full cycles (EFC) separate from microcycle count; compute EFC with proper throughput definition but DO NOT infer from experiment lacking Ah.
P17-E: for industrial BAT data, C20/CCA/DCA/SOH and gassing all different endpoints; no “best carbon” without pre-specified multi-objective metrics and cross-validated confidence interval. Analyze reproducibility, replicates per formulation; authors' abstract often lacks these details.

## 7. Conclusions (C67–C75)
C67 — Carbon NAM benefits in HRPSoC are supported, but conductive, crystal-growth and double-layer mechanisms co-exist and may not uniquely separate for one battery; S02/S07/S09.
C68 — Higher carbon external area improved normalized short-term CA from 0.21 to 0.58 A/Ah under S01, with plateau beyond 92.1 m2/g; 2V 2.5Ah NAM-limited scope only.
C69 — Short-term DCA rank did not remain quantitative over many weeks and an airport-parking rest in S01; no inference “increased DCA today ⇒ lasting same ranking”.
C70 — Carbon-foam dose effect nonmonotonic: S03 1% CF =3642 vs blank1267 microcycles while 1.5%=2077 and 2%=1803 in specified 2C/50% SOC protocol; not universal 1% recommendation.
C71 — NCC group 4324 vs AC1834 vs blank809 microcycles in S06; absolute cycle counts and ratios not comparable across S03 vs S06.
C72 — Extra carbon can increase HER, self-discharge, water loss and lower efficiency; VRLA oxygen cycle further complicates balance; S02/S09. Material surface/impurities matter.
C73 — Lignosulfonate/BaSO4 and carbon have separate, interacting mechanisms; synthetic lignin outperformed other additives for particular S08 HRPSoC cells, carbon not solitary cause.
C74 — PAM carbon effects require separate study; historical sources emphasized weak/unreliable PAM benefits, while 2025–26 studies observe selected improvements under special composition, not a universal contradiction or license to retrofit. S02/S10/S11/S12.
C75 — DCA is a measure of transient acceptance; it is not C20, CCA, water-loss rate or long-term durability, even with robust EIS correlation. S01/S04/S05/S13.

## 8. Testable hypotheses (H43–H48)
H43 — Fixed-dose carbon external surface predicts short-term DCA in NAM-limited cells only until transport/other-plate limits cause saturation; validate replicated factorial data.
H44 — Carbon amount has nonmonotonic optimum for HRPSoC microcycles because benefits compete with pore transport/HER and formation; validate replicated independent batches & gassing.
H45 — Combined carbon+lignosulfonate+BaSO4 design explains cycle result more than carbon identity alone; matched randomized 2V cells.
H46 — Predicting aging-run-in DCA from one early DCA test has systematically overstated generalizability; check weeks-long measured cycles and held-out manufacturer.
H47 — PAM carbon improvements can have distinct oxidative stability/gassing tradeoffs; independently verify 2025–26 AGM claims with water-loss and C20 controls.
H48 — Adding carbon to existing electrolyte or nonfactory Pb active mass cannot be inferred safe/effective from factory-electrode studies; lack of equivalent experiments means unsupported retrofit, not an implementable charging intervention.
