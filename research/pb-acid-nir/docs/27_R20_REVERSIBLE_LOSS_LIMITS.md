# R20-A04 — Reversible capacity loss, long-term field follow-up and limits of regeneration
Date 2026-11-28; PARTIAL. Literature only; no equipment operation or new charger settings.

## A04-S01 Wagner / Sauer 2001
R. Wagner, D.U. Sauer, Charge strategies for valve-regulated lead/acid batteries in solar power applications, J Power Sources 95:141–152, PII S0378775300006145. Publisher https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378775300006145 ; full machine-text mirror https://www.academia.edu/18165118/Charge_strategies_for_valve_regulated_lead_acid_batteries_in_solar_power_applications . Original PDF not verified in this pass. Field project begun spring 1998; Talhof tubular GEL nominal 300Ah C10; four individually charged series sections, common discharge. Separate 16 small systems (eight AGM, eight GEL). Every six months sequential capacity tests after ordinary solar charge and intensive charge. Seasonal difference particularly pronounced after winter. End-of-charge voltage alone did not guarantee full charge. C10, not C20. Same research lineage as R20-A03 Fraunhofer annual report; not independent replication. Sequential tests cannot isolate charge completeness, reversible sulfate, redistribution and conditioning effects.

## A04-S02 Bopp et al., 2010

Thus relative +155% at 85d still only ~47% nominal, and at 120d essentially no recovery. Fig.7 mass trends suggest water loss; authors estimate 15g ~35% water loss. ## A04-S03 Hund, Sandia, 1998
Tom Hund, Capacity Loss in PV Batteries and Recovery Procedures, Sandia National Laboratories. Author document PDF mirror https://www.zetatalk3.com/docs/Batteries/Lead_Acid/Capacity_Loss_in_PV_Batteries_and_Recovery_Procedures_1998.pdf ; visual Fig.4 p.7 verified. One flooded golf-cart lead-antimony battery after two years normal PV and six months cycling at low SOC: roughly 600Ah cumulative input, final 151Ah (~80% original); specific gravity returned near expected value. This is large input and only a final capacity endpoint, not incremental capacity after each charge. Cannot infer coulombic recovery efficiency or optimal stopping time from SG alone. Repeated high-temperature laboratory treatment not transferable to modern automotive batteries. The report discusses multiple degradation mechanisms including stratification, gas pockets, PAM/grid contact and sulfate; not all PCL is sulfation. Independent lineage from Fraunhofer, but single case and no matched control.

## A04-S04 Canada field case, 2019
Opportunity for Improving Lead-Acid Battery Management ... Energies 12(12), 2237, DOI 10.3390/en12122237, full publisher HTML https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/12/12/2237 , §§3–5. One 48V 1000Ah flooded bank among eight monitored installations. 30s U/I/T/Ah logs. Effective usable capacity ~150Ah during winter undercharging; after several controller changes performance and cell SG improved over three months. Two cells initially ~1.10 vs others ~1.25, later all >1.30. No standardized final capacity discharge performed; cannot claim restored 1000Ah or isolate effect of any single change. Authors note extended recovery charging inappropriate permanently. Ah accumulator drift due to parasitic reactions makes positive net Ah and tail current insufficient proof of full SOC. Not a calibrated C20 or automotive transfer.

## Operational research criterion (not an implemented charging algorithm)
Define a reference capacity Qref under a fixed applicable method and safe manufacturer-permitted preparation, Qwork under the working preparation, and Delta=Qref-Qwork. Delta describes operationally recoverable difference under the tested protocols; not uniquely sulfate mass. Residual capacity deficit relative to nominal/baseline after reference preparation is residual under that test, not proof of permanently irreversible chemistry. Assess per-battery safety and independent functional endpoints, including CCA only for starter batteries and self-discharge/weak-cell evidence when applicable.
For staged historical records, incremental gain g_i=Qref_i-Qref_(i-1), uncertainty of g_i from paired measurements, and incremental throughput/energy/time. Distinguish NOT_ESTABLISHED (interval overlaps zero) from EQUIVALENT_SMALL_GAIN (upper confidence bound below prespecified meaningful gain). Plateau does not prove all sulfate removed or permanent failure; assess absolute functional adequacy and risks separately. Stop/avoid additional treatment when abnormal heating/short/damage or manufacturer limits apply, irrespective of gain. No numeric universal tail-current, voltage, Ah or stage count; no physical testing authorized. Further study: prospective safe matched ordinary-charge control, repeated reference tests, water/gas/thermal observations and post-withdrawal persistence.

C107 — S01/S02 seasonal gap up to 20% between two C10 preparations; terminal charge voltage alone not sufficient. Same cohort, not independent.
C108 — S02 selected cells 2001–2008: two maintain/high capacity while others show dendrites, one zero capacity; high cell capacity not whole-pack health. Monitoring gap and no long-term control.
C109 — S02 81 GEL 3×3×3×3 storage factorial demonstrates strong time/T dependence of residual recoverability; hot prolonged storage exhibits near-zero or incomplete recovery. Not automotive C20.
C110 — S02 relative gain and final adequacy differ; Fig.6 ~0.22→0.56Ah still <half nominal; water loss association not unique mechanism; zero gain at zero capacity is not success.
C111 — S03 cumulative input near 600Ah and final 151Ah demonstrate SG normalization need not restore original capacity; no incremental capacity series or optimal stopping proof.
C112 — S04 usable capacity and SG recovered in one field bank but no standardized final capacity measurement; multiple changes, no causal separation; Ah drift and tail current not full-SOC proof.
H68 — Difference between standardized reference and working-preparation capacity tracks reversible operational undercharge independently of some aging modes; validate matched conditions.
H69 — Declining incremental capacity gain plus uncertainty and independent safety/functional metrics better identifies diminishing returns than tail current alone; test archived matched records.
H70 — Water loss and internal short mechanisms can dominate residual capacity deficit despite improved charge completeness; validate independent mass/diagnostic evidence.
