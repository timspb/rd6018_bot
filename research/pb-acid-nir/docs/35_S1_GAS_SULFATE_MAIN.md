C140 — Nine flooded production lots show 3.85-fold normalized water loss variation, associated with both electrode Te proxies; no independent causal isolation.
C141 — VRLA oxygen recombination can persist at high negative polarization in one modified AGM; pressure is indirect and no universal gas threshold follows.
