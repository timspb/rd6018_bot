Draft test design: select serviceable flooded specimens of same model/lot/history; no altered or defective cells, no VRLA opening, no unapproved charging. ## Critical review of R09-K (documentary only)
Cross-check R09 report and reports 34–37: K concerns distinguishing three physical goals and practical claims, not establishing a universal operating profile. (1) Destratification: reduce spatial density variation within a cell; S12 comparative density outcome and S13 independent local measurements. (2) Equalization: reduce differences between cells; requires per-cell state and separate test, not established by intra-cell mixing. (3) Recharge: restore reversible charge; needs independent standardized discharge, not inferred from SG or returned Ah. S10 demonstrates pore-scale restrictions may coexist with gas-driven bulk movement. S12 reported water estimate cannot support safety or resource equivalence. TG105526/TG105607 observations do not establish causality or best duration; no independent reproduction. On these bounded questions the documentary distinction is established, efficacy of specific MIX remains UNKNOWN, and no threshold follows. R09-K PASS; R09-Q remains OPEN under original full-protocol/water criterion, R09-P OPEN pending target-model methodology/metrology. This is not physical or firmware acceptance.
