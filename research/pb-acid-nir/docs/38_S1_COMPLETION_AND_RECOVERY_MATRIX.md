## Protocol semantics and uncertainty
For future supervised research only: one specified battery model and revision per stratum, ordinary manufacturer-approved preparation and comparator; fixed temperature/rest/cutoff/rate, calibrated voltage/current/time/temperature. Reference capacity is a functional endpoint conditional on preparation, not direct chemical SOC. Record actual terminal CV, current limit, external current and measurement validity independently; distinguish profile completed, target not established, invalid data, safety stop. Compare on independently assigned matched batteries with baseline and followup reference capacity, record all exclusions. For recovery use difference-in-differences D=(Qpost-Qpre)treatment-(Qpost-Qpre)control. u(D)^2=c^T Cov(Q)c, c=(1,-1,-1,1) for vector (Qtpost,Qtpre,Qcpost,Qcpre); use measured repeatability/covariance and separately biological variability, not nominal accuracy. Predefine practical delta_min and confidence coverage before intervention. Lower bound >delta_min = evidence of meaningful benefit; entire interval within [-delta_min,+delta_min] = evidence of small difference; otherwise inconclusive. Safety or protocol limit terminates with reason, not success. Reference retention after standardized storage/cycling and functional test; chemistry assay needed for mechanism. No experimental permission, no actual delta_min or sample size fixed; pilot and model-specific bounds still required.
Executed tools/s1_completion_uncertainty.py: two original PDFs verified; synthetic efficiency examples (integral .9783/.9314/.8197 vs marginal .5/.25) illustrate distinct quantities. Repeated R20-A02 synthetic 1.44Ah uncertainty check (not new experimental evidence): independent paired 95% halfwidth 3.991Ah, difference-in-differences 5.645Ah, excludes between-battery variance. A gain of 4Ah is not necessarily evidence of comparative recovery. No charging parameters derived.
