Early 200A block reached prescribed cap, not demonstrated failure at 1000; final voltage limit reached at 305. Earlier/later ambient temperature differed ~7C; internal rise above ambient ~9→12C. 400A comparison at ~40C reported; age and prior cycling confounded. Two cells high near end, returned within specification after recharge. No ultimate EOL, no independent causal assignment to sulfation, no aged repair trial. 
At final check, repeated reference cycles yielded 851Ah@32C,953@37C,977@38C; authors' approximate 1%/C correction produces 851/905/918Ah, +7.87% after two additional cycles even after their correction. Correction not independently validated for this bank; preserve their numbers, no universal temperature coefficient. Within-bank preparation and temperature matter. Fig2 lower ten-hour capacity ~91% conventional product spec but similar high-rate capacity; cannot isolate carbon from grid design. This is a prevention/maintenance benchmark, not proof of restoration. Key counterexample: stable reference capacity can coexist with worse charge acceptance/shorter PSoC interval; no single SOH scalar establishes functional life.
## S05 INL/EXT-12-28003, UltraBattery report
Official PDF https://avt.inl.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/hev/UltraBatteryReport.pdf ; §4.3.1 text printed pp29–30, Figs15/17/19; screenshot 403 and local download 403. Three separate Furukawa UltraBattery 12V modules, SOC windows 43–53,53–63,63–73%, simulated Honda Civic hybrid profile. Number of profile repetitions between simulated engine recharges declined 9→5, 8→4, 6→3, then returned to 9,8,6 following full recharge/capacity test respectively. Relative declines 44.4%,50%,50%; rebounds 80%,100%,100% relative to reduced baseline. These are repetitions of drive profile, not independent batteries, Ah capacity or cycle-life EOL. Intervention combines full recharge and reference test, no concurrent no-intervention control. Supports reversibility of operational limitation in specific modules, not a special waveform or persistent improvement after withdrawal. Simulated mileage is not measured service mileage; do not adopt proposed recharge interval.
## S06 Alipour/Nasseri 2025, Next Research 2(3) 100399
DOI 10.1016/j.nexres.2025.100399, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S3050475925002696 ; abstract and publisher-indexed methodology/results snippets only; full article access 403. 697 12V200Ah FT AGM VRLA from warehouse storage >18 months, capacity screening by reported I10/10h discharge and cutoff 10.8V. Approximately half reportedly retained capacity; authors claim industrial recovery efficiency 72%, target 90–100% rated capacity. Exact recovered population denominator, group allocation, repeat test, water/structure evidence and followup inaccessible. Do not compute 697*.72 recovered count, or interpret 72% as mean capacity regain. Storage loss differs from field-aged corrosion/shedding. No transfer to automotive EFB or approval of voltages. Large sample is not controlled longitudinal evidence.
