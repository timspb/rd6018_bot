## Comparative design constraints
For future approved studies, separate factory material prevention and aged battery restoration cohorts. Independently matched ordinary-charge controls, predeclared waveform/Q+/Q-/Wh+/Wh-/elapsed time and thermal history, verified measurement bandwidth and polarity, independent reference-capacity endpoints and retention, gas/water/thermal risk plus all exclusions. Where equal-time and equal-net-charge comparisons conflict use separate estimands rather than claim equality of both. New reference cells not control for restoration of used cells. No numerical model-specific bounds, sample size or experimental authorization; no P gates advanced.
