## S2 methodological result
Executed tools/s2_waveform_metrology.py on explicitly synthetic traces. Sign convention positive I charges, positive terminal U; simultaneous samples, piecewise linear interpolation of U and I separately. Split intervals at I zero crossing, integrate Q+ and Q- as magnitudes in Ah, W+ and W- as magnitudes in Wh. Exact integral of product of two linear functions over segment h (hours): h*(2UaIa+UaIb+UbIa+2UbIb)/6. Qnet=Q+−Q-; Wnet=W+−W-. Input with nonfinite data, nonincreasing time, interval above predeclared gap limit or nonpositive U rejected. These checks are for ordinary-polarity energy accounting, not for diagnosing reversal. No interpolation across unknown gaps. No inference of energy balance from average current alone.
Synthetic checks: sign crossing +2→−2A at 12V for 1h yields .5/.5Ah and 6/6Wh; linear U10→14 and I0→6 gives 3Ah/38Wh (product of averages gives 36Wh); linear U14→10 and I+2→−2 gives Qnet0 and Wnet4/3Wh. These are artificial numerical cases, not charging recommendations. Phase-locked samples of artificial I=1+sin(2πt/10) at t=10k s estimate mean I correctly, but mean-square 1 vs actual1.5A², −33.3%; shows aliasing of inferred resistive loss even when integrated Ah is accurate. Does not model a particular battery or instrument. Need separately established anti-aliasing, synchronized sampling and bandwidth sufficient for actual measured waveform and uncertainty target, cross-check against calibrated integrating reference.
## Comparative/retention analysis plan
Predefine one specific model and allowed profile and measurement limits, ordinary manufacturer-approved charge as matched control; random assignment within baseline-matched pairs of independent specimens (not repeated cycles as replicates). Independent standardized reference discharge with fixed rest/T/rate/cutoff before and after and at predeclared calendar/throughput followups. Same reference-test schedule both groups: testing itself can condition batteries. Distinguish estimands (matched elapsed time, matched net Ah, matched final functional charge) because all may not be attainable at once. Measure Q+/Q-/W+/W-, true RMS, temperature, water/gas where safely measurable and independent functional endpoints, preserve exclusions and raw quality flags. Define main pair-level contrast D=(post−pre)treatment−(post−pre)control. Positive lower confidence bound above predeclared useful effect required for meaningful benefit; equivalence interval wholly within ±margin for small differences; otherwise inconclusive. A separate no-harm gate requires valid safety endpoints; safety stop is not success or silently removed observation.
Power-planning illustration only: two-sided normal approximation alpha .05, power .8, n=ceil(((1.959964+.841621)*SD_pair/effect_above_null)^2). SD 2/4/8Ah, effects 2/4Ah give respectively (8,2),(32,8),(126,32) independent pairs. Very small n not adequate for actual design without variance-estimation/t-distribution adjustment. Variance includes between-specimen plus measurement uncertainty, not just instrument accuracy. Actual useful effect, dispersion, dropout and observation horizon need model-specific pilot and approval. Failure = predefined observed endpoint; administrative stop = right-censoring; safety stop separately reported/competing event or composite endpoint predeclared. No fitted survival curves from one-bank observations. R03-P/R19-P/R17-P/R18-P still OPEN; no physical permission. S2 main deliverable assembled with remaining named open gates; move to S3.
