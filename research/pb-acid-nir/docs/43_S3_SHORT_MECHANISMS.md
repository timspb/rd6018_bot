## Diagnostic evidence matrix
- Dendritic bridge: independently verified metallic phase + connected path + associated electrical conductance/short; gray spots and CT morphology alone insufficient for CONFIRMED mechanism. No intentionally induced defects.
- Mechanical/contact short: independently observed damaged separator, displaced grid or conductive sediment/contact; same functional weakness and parasitic current possible. Yuasa2011 teardown adds distinct mechanism evidence.
- Chemical self-discharge: external leakage excluded, independently measured retention with equal preparation and T, chemical attribution only with independent evidence.
- Relaxation: U change at nominal zero current can reflect polarization/SG/T, not independently measured loss of stored charge. OPzS strict rest screen no eligible hour (report42); no calibration from that archive.
- External leakage: measured terminal circuit load, distinguish from internal paths; unknown current or stale samples invalidate conclusion. Functional risk, measurement validity and mechanism confidence separate; if suspected internal fault stop experimental charge and seek qualified assessment, not a treatment recommendation.
