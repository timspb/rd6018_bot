- MEASURED vs ESTIMATED vs UNKNOWN: report Q(I,T,cutoff), CCA, DCA, resistance, SOC and SOH_C separately; no scalar substitute. C31–39, C127–133, C172, C179. Test metadata and calibration before any label; unknown target C20 stays UNKNOWN.

- MIX/stratification and inter-cell equalization separate, not justified by label alone. C10, C138, C147–149. Need model-specific allowed mode and independent spatial evidence, water/temperature risk. No generic voltage/duration or transfer flooded→VRLA.
- Recovery efficacy: matched ordinary charge and independent pre/post/retention reference Q, measured signed Ah/Wh and thermal history. C100–105, C154–160, C165. No superiority claim from pulse frequency or one improvement.
- Diagnostic confidence and safety separate. C167–171, C183–185. Fault suspicion cannot be dismissed by pack voltage or normal resistance; uncertain mechanism is not permission to continue a risky intervention. No automatic defect treatment.
These are documentation and measurement requirements only. Current RD6018/ESP/BV-600 capabilities not audited in this sprint, no claim of implementation defect or recommendation to alter runtime. Addressed recommendation packet after protocol review, no deployment.
