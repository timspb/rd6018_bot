## B. Coup de fouet (CDF) literature
2002 VRLA study https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378775302003166 indexed sections: trough and plateau depend on current, T, previous DOD, float duration/voltage, aging. Reference electrode experiments show negative and positive contribution; mechanism not settled. 2006 study https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S037877530501565X indexed abstract plus cited abstract in 2002 page: rest and SOC/DOD confound capacity inference. 2025 modeling alternative https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352152X25014082 indexed summary only, not decisive.
Rys et al. 2021 J Power Technologies101(2)112–126 https://papers.itc.pw.edu.pl/index.php/JPT/article/download/1741/1307 full indexed text; screenshots pp122/123 failed cache miss. Paired groups, second experiment 6 small 12V units of 3 constructions (Varta4Ah, Panasonic3.4Ah), 55C accelerated ageing, C/1 discharge, C/10 recharge, periodic float, not field car batteries. Table2/3 and conclusion: no robust Q mapping, time from trough to plateau sometimes tracks aging/time better than Q. Repeatability failed in isolated controlled conditions; later short discharges after first did not reproduce effect, one-week intervals did. Do not infer precise sulfation mass or full SOC from presence/absence; candidate feature only. Differentiate classic initial dip/recovery under constant load from multi-hour sag, current transitions, instrument artifact, cell imbalance, short, etc.
