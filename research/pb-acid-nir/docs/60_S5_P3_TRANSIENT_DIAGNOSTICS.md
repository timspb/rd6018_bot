# S5 report60 — P3 transient feature protocol and abstention
2026-11-29. P3 documentary design, not hardware acceptance, not a treatment. Canon D07–D10; no production RD6018, ESP, node104 or BV-600 changes. Prior evidence R05/R06/R07/R12/R13, reports32/43/45/55/56/57/59, S013–S015. Literature conclusions and evidence grades unchanged. Do not claim a synthetic fixture is an electrochemical model.
## P3a: common input and provenance
Use P1/P2 metrology and model-specific approved preparation as prerequisites. Freeze specimen_id, model/lot, flooded/AGM/GEL and grid alloy separately, history, charge procedure, measured starting reference Q/SOC method, rest elapsed, terminal Kelvin U, signed I (positive charging), battery/proxy temperature and location, synchronized monotonic clock, actual physical load transition t0, known channel delays and uncertainty; archive unrounded samples and all rejected runs. A commanded transition is not proof of a measured current transition. Qualify channel bandwidth, time response, calibration and external leakage before comparing early windows. No risky tests on damaged/suspect batteries; no intentional short/reversal or VRLA opening; model-specific manufacturer limits and independent shutdown remain prerequisites. A trace feature never controls safety or charging termination.
## P3b: relaxation after charge or discharge
Separate charge-OFF from discharge-OFF histories. Candidate windows at 1/5/10/30/60/120/300/600s, extended to hours where appropriate, chosen and frozen after measurement-only pilot. Measure voltage in bounded windows, not the unobserved instantaneous voltage. Record actual preceding current, duration and signed Ah/Wh, actual residual current throughout rest, temperature change; reject missing coverage, unstable preceding/zero-current phase and temperature drift. R_app(t)=(U_pre−U_rest(t))/I_pre includes polarization and history, not pure ohmic R; preserve sign and do not force positive. Extract finite window differences; no extrapolated equilibrium or time constants unless independently identified. Use independently measured reference capacity and independent SOC preparation. Compare leakage-free vs observed external standby load only within approved conditions; no deliberate defect. Reference electrode and spatial SG data only qualified laboratory/accessible flooded method, separately graded.
## P3c: coup de fouet under load
Preregister baseline interval, settling exclusion, actual current amplitude/tolerance, sample gap and bandwidth, fixed-width time bins, final late-window length, minimum measurable recovery and maximum temperature span. Use median in each adequately covered bin; uneven sample cadence can bias unweighted medians, so qualify uniform sampling/time alignment and reject incompatible recordings. Initial decline, trough bin and subsequent recovery must exceed the measurement bounds; use a conservative 2× absolute voltage-error bound for a difference (not a statistical CI). Do not interpret median-bin trough as exact instantaneous minimum; exclude unresolved early transition if trough falls in first analyzed bin. Final window is observed late behavior, not equilibrium plateau. Distinguish candidate single dip, insufficient resolution, ambiguous amplitude, multiple dips/drift, recovery without stable late window, invalid trace. Do not infer no CDF exists outside window. Current changes/load OFF rebound, missing samples and temperature drift invalidate comparison; residual electrical load should be measured independently. Research pilot targets 10Hz or better near transition and slower long windows, not a claim of BV-600 capability. Actual time bins from pilot and preregistration, no universal amplitude or time threshold. CDF is a discharge feature, retrospective previous-charge assessment remains hypothesis, not direct MAIN/MIX termination.
## P3d: diagnostic identifiability and independent validation
| Feature | What it can measure | What is not independently established |
|---|---|---|
| Rest U(t), finite differences | Change in terminal voltage at known history/current/T | Unique sulfate amount, short current, full SOC |
| Apparent DC resistance | Protocol-dependent terminal response | Separation of grid/contact/pore/kinetic resistance |
| CDF | Shape under stable load, bounded voltage recovery | Unique sulfate mechanism, full charge or C20 |
| EIS | Complex response at calibrated amplitude/frequency | Unique equivalent circuit parameters/physical degradation |
| Charge acceptance | Current/charge admitted in fixed protocol | C20 or reversible treatment benefit |
| Retained reference Q | Functional capacity under declared conditions | Physical cause of degradation |
| SG(z) | Accessible electrolyte gradient | Pore acid uniformity or VRLA applicability |
Independent mechanisms need corroborating phase/morphology/conductivity/gradient evidence from qualified laboratories. Functional estimates may be validated without assigning mechanism. Unknown or mixed mechanism explicit; no safety reassurance from a normal trace. R06 EIS requires separately calibrated analyzer, open/short/load fixture qualification where appropriate, repeat spectra, raw frequency/real/imag, phase/sign, temperature, SOC/time, linearity and stationarity; no reuse of DC sampling as EIS. R13 retention: exclude external loads, identical preparation and safe reference discharge before/after fixed storage, no attribution to internal short without independent evidence.
## P3e: pre-registration and evaluation
Unit = independent physical battery, blocks by construction/model/batch/age and baseline Q. Pilot characterizes instrument variance and repeated-measure conditioning; pilot specimens excluded from confirmatory holdout. Match SOC/T/history/current; rest arms 10min/1h/4h/24h illustrative only, reset approved preparation between probes, randomize feasible order and keep repeated tests as clustered observations. Comparator: simple OCV/T and Rdc predictor frozen before holdout, compare additional CDF/relaxation against same eligible batteries. Primary endpoint battery-level paired absolute reference-Q error difference (Ah and nominal pp); use battery-level confidence interval, minimum practically useful gain chosen before data, sample size from independent pilot variability; secondary coverage/abstention, false reassurance for low Q, worst-case error and model/type strata. Report every invalid/ambiguous trace, coverage denominator and fixed fallback, don't silently drop hard cases. Model selection and scaling on training only, no leakage of measured Q into blind predictor or SOC metadata. No validated threshold until independent cohort. For mechanism classification use separately verified mechanism labels, not predictions from other electrical features; assess sensitivity/specificity/coverage and unknowns separately. Negative results and conditional effectiveness retained. Proposed protocol does not close empirical gates R05-K/R06-K/R12-K.
